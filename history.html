Our Journey

A legacy of innovation, milestones, and record-breaking efficiency.

2023

First Place National Title

Our prototype vehicle, Ventus, achieved an astounding 3,587 MPG at the Shell Eco-Marathon, securing the 1st place title in North America.

2021

A New Era: Aurum

The team unveiled Aurum, our first-ever Urban Concept vehicle, designed with practical road features while still pushing the limits of fuel efficiency.

2018

Carbon Fiber Innovation

The team successfully designed and manufactured its first full carbon-fiber monocoque chassis, dramatically reducing vehicle weight and setting a new internal standard.

2015

Breaking 1,000 MPG

A milestone achievement as the team's vehicle officially broke the 1,000 MPG barrier at competition for the first time in team history.
